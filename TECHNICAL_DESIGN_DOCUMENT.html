Executive Summary

Nivasa is an enterprise residential operating platform designed for Indian cooperative housing societies and high-rise apartment complexes. It replaces fragmented paper registers, disparate spreadsheets, and unmonitored WhatsApp groups with a unified, high-integrity system.

The platform addresses five foundational societal workflows: automated maintenance billing with an absolute integer-paise standard (zero floating-point drift), deterministic 7-evidence bank statement reconciliation, hybrid AI grievance triage with deterministic life-safety emergency overrides, DPDP Act 2023-compliant gatekeeper kiosk kiosks with offline synchronization, and an executive committee oversight dashboard.

1. System Architecture & Technology Justification

1.1 The Modular Monolith

Nivasa is architected as a Modular Monolith structured under a single monorepo (@nivasa/shared, @nivasa/backend, @nivasa/frontend).

Why Modular Monolith Over Microservices:
Residential society finance requires atomic relational consistency across invoices, receipts, and flat ledgers. A distributed microservices architecture introduces distributed transaction failure modes (two-phase commit latency, saga rollbacks), which are unacceptable for audited housing society records. A modular monolith guarantees ACID guarantees inside PostgreSQL transactions while preserving clear domain boundaries.
Layer Technology Architectural Rationale
Language TypeScript 5.x (Strict) End-to-end type safety spanning database entities, REST responses, and UI forms.
Frontend React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS Sub-second HMR, high rendering performance for large bank reconciliation queues, and responsive tablet UI.

2. Relational Data Model & Financial Mathematics

2.1 The Integer-Paise Standard

Financial calculations never use JavaScript Number floating-point arithmetic. All balances, late fees, and rates are stored as integer paise (1 INR = 100 paise). An 1,150 sq ft flat billed at ₹6.50/sq ft computes as 1150 × 650 = 747,500 paise (₹7,475.00), preventing any fractional rounding loss.

3. Application Workflows & AI Implementation

3.1 Bank Statement Reconciliation Engine

Bank statements (HDFC, ICICI, SBI CSV exports) are parsed with zero silent row discards. A deterministic 7-evidence matching algorithm evaluates candidate invoices:

  • Tier 1 (+40%): Exact payment reference string match in bank narrative.
  • Tier 2 (+25%): Verified resident UPI ID match.
  • Tier 3 (+20%): Tower and flat identifier match (e.g. "T07-1203").
  • Tier 4 (+15%): Exact amount matching invoice outstanding balance.
  • Tier 5 & 6 (+10% each): Due-date proximity and past resident payment method.
  • Ambiguity Protection: If multiple candidate flats match the same score or amount, automatic posting is halted, and the transaction is placed into NEEDS_REVIEW for human accounting review.

3.2 Complaint AI Triage & Deterministic Safety Escalation

Grievances undergo a dual-stage safety and intelligence pipeline:

Deterministic Safety Rule Engine (Priority 1):
To prevent dangerous AI hallucinations, complaints mentioning "gas smell", "sparks / fire", "lift stuck with passenger", or "electric shock" immediately override AI classification, assign CRITICAL urgency, dispatch an alert to the facility duty manager, and lock out automated closure.

For non-emergency complaints, the Google Gemini AI Adapter categorizes the issue (Plumbing, Electrical, Lift, Security, etc.), infers urgency, and recommends a maintenance technician with transparent reasoning. If external AI services are offline, a local keyword fallback engine seamlessly assumes triage duties with no disruption.

3.3 Gatekeeper Tablet & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

  • Touchscreen Ergonomics: High-contrast 64px touch targets and dedicated PIN keypad designed for low-cost outdoor gate tablets.
  • Offline Queue: Local IndexedDB queue buffers arrivals when society Wi-Fi drops and synchronizes idempotently via client UUIDs upon reconnection.
  • 90-Day Privacy Purge: Physical visitor photo files are permanently expunged from disk after 90 days in compliance with statutory Indian data minimization laws.

4. UI/UX Design Decisions

Nivasa departs from generic, harsh SaaS aesthetics in favor of a Warm Editorial Design System:

  • Color Palette: Deep Walnut (#2c221e) and Warm Sand (#faf8f5) establish an inviting, authoritative atmosphere that minimizes glare for elderly residents.
  • Visual Density: High-density data tables with monospaced numbers (tabular-nums) for accounting staff; spacious, friendly card layouts for residents.
  • Accessibility: WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast compliance across all text elements and interactive form controls.

5. Scope Adjustments & Future Roadmap

5.1 What Was Deliberately Cut

  • Video Intercom via WebRTC: Cut in favor of fast 6-digit PIN passes to prevent gate queue congestion.
  • Automated License Plate Recognition (ANPR): Cut due to high hardware camera dependency and non-standard Indian plate fonts.
  • Full Double-Entry Balance Sheet General Ledger: Flat ledgers and dues ageing were prioritized over depreciation schedules.

5.2 Future Roadmap (Phase 2)

  • Live Dynamic UPI QR Webhooks: Real-time payment confirmation via Razorpay/Cashfree webhook callbacks.
  • Multi-Gate WebSocket Cluster: Instant sync across North, South, and Service gates using Redis pub/sub.
  • Multilingual Voice Dictation: Speech-to-complaint logging in Hindi and Marathi using Whisper and Gemini.
  • IoT Sub-Meter Telemetry: Automated digital water and DG power meter logging into monthly invoices.
